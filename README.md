## 🟣 I Don't Start With the Stack. I Start With the Problem.

A team is repeating the same workflow every day.

A founder has a product idea but no clear technical path.

Business data lives across spreadsheets, APIs, emails, dashboards, and disconnected tools.

A company wants to use AI — but doesn't need another chatbot.

**That's where I like to work.**

For **3+ years**, I've worked across frontend, backend, databases, APIs, AI workflows, infrastructure, and product delivery.

But the question I care about most is:

> ### How can software and AI solve a measurable business problem?

My background includes both a **BBA and MBA**, so I naturally think about engineering from both the **technical** and **business** side.

I don't only ask:

> **Can we build this?**

I also ask:

✅ What business problem are we solving?  
✅ What manual work can we eliminate?  
✅ Where is the operational bottleneck?  
✅ Who will actually use the system?  
✅ How does it fit into the existing workflow?  
✅ Where can it fail?  
✅ Can it scale with the business?  
✅ What measurable value does it create?

## 🟣 Business Thinking × Engineering

My business education influences the way I approach software.

I don't see engineering as only:

```text
Requirement
    ↓
  Code
    ↓
 Deploy
```

I think more like:

```text
Business Problem
       ↓
Understand the Workflow
       ↓
Find the Real Bottleneck
       ↓
Design the System
       ↓
Build the Product
       ↓
Measure the Outcome
       ↓
Improve the System
```

### ◆ What this changes in practice

✅ Understand the business before choosing technology  
✅ Think about ROI, efficiency, and operational outcomes  
✅ Consider how people actually use the system  
✅ Avoid unnecessary technical complexity  
✅ Think about maintenance and ownership after launch  
✅ Connect engineering decisions to measurable outcomes  

> ### The goal isn't more software.
> ### The goal is a better-running business because of the software.

## 🤖 How I Think About AI

I'm most interested in AI when it has **a defined responsibility inside a dependable system**.

Not this:

```text
Prompt
  ↓
 AI
  ↓
Hope
```

More like this:

```text
Business Event
      ↓
Validate Input
      ↓
Collect Context
      ↓
Retrieve Data
      ↓
Apply Business Rules
      ↓
Use AI Where Judgment Helps
      ↓
Validate Output
      ↓
Human Approval When Necessary
      ↓
Update The System
      ↓
Monitor / Retry / Recover
```

That's the difference between adding AI as a novelty and engineering AI as part of a real workflow.
